# Code of conduct

Cairn is made by people who give their time. Treat everyone here the way
you would want to be treated.

- Be kind and assume good intent. Disagree about ideas, never about people.
- Welcome newcomers and questions; nobody starts out knowing everything.
- No harassment, insults, or discrimination of any kind, in any channel the
  project uses.

If someone breaks these rules, tell the maintainers at
`conduct@example.invalid` (HUMAN: the real address). Reports are kept
confidential. Maintainers may remove comments, code or people that do not
follow this code.
