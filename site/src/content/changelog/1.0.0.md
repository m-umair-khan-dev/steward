title: Steward 1.0
summary: The first release: a file manager for Ubuntu that keeps folders tidy.
---

- **Tidy** sorts a messy folder into kind folders after showing you the plan, and one Undo puts everything back.
- **Find anything** with Ctrl+K: folders and files by name, ranked as you type, with Peek on Space.
- **Folder sizes** on every folder, worked out in the background.
- **Dual pane** with F3: copy or move across with F5 and F6.
- **Workspaces** gather folders and files from anywhere on one page. Nothing moves.
- **Free Up Space** shows what is safe to clear and why. Everything goes to the Trash, and Undo brings it back.
- **Installed** lists what apt, Snap, Flatpak and the language tools installed, with why each package is there.
- **About This Computer** shows battery health, storage, memory, displays and security at a glance.
