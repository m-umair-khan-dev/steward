Open Preferences with <kbd>Ctrl+,</kbd>.

- **General**: the default view, hidden files, which folder counts as Downloads, and **Use as default file manager** — folders open in Cairn, and other apps' "Show in folder" comes to Cairn while it runs. Turn it off to give folders back to Files.
- **Appearance**: light, dark or the system's style; Cairn's purple accent or the system's; card size. Animations follow the system's *Reduce Animation* setting.
- **Shortcuts**: every key, as below.
- **Search and Index**: pause indexing, pause on battery, leave folders out, rebuild.
