---
title: Dual pane
description: Two folders side by side.
order: 13
section: Tools
---

Press <kbd>F3</kbd> to split the window. The active pane is underlined; <kbd>Tab</kbd> switches. <kbd>F5</kbd> copies the selection to the other pane and <kbd>F6</kbd> moves it — both with Undo. <kbd>Ctrl+U</kbd> swaps the two folders. Both come back the next time you open Cairn.
