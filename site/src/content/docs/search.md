---
title: Find anything
description: Search every folder and file name as you type.
order: 4
section: Everyday
---

Press <kbd>Ctrl+K</kbd> and type part of a name. Results are ranked as you type: a name that starts with what you typed comes before one that merely contains it, and folders you use come first.

- <kbd>↑</kbd> <kbd>↓</kbd> move, <kbd>Enter</kbd> opens.
- <kbd>Space</kbd> peeks at the result.
- <kbd>Ctrl+Enter</kbd> shows it in its folder, selected.

Search uses Cairn's index, kept up to date in the background at the lowest priority. In **Preferences › Search and Index** you can pause it, pause it on battery, leave folders out, or rebuild it.
