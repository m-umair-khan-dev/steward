---
title: Peek
description: Look at a file without opening an app.
order: 5
section: Everyday
---

Select a file and press <kbd>Space</kbd>. Pictures, PDFs (page by page), text and code, audio and video open in a quick view over the window. <kbd>←</kbd> and <kbd>→</kbd> move to the next file; <kbd>Space</kbd> or <kbd>Esc</kbd> closes. *Open* hands the file to its usual app; *Info* shows its details.
