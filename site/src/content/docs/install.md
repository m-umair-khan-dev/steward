---

Cairn is packaged for Ubuntu. The [download page](/download/) has the commands that add its signing key and repository; after that, `sudo apt install cairn` installs it and updates arrive with your other updates.

The first time Cairn starts, a short welcome shows a messy folder being sorted, four keys worth knowing, and a few choices (light or dark, accent colour, card size). Skip it at any time; it does not come back. After an update, Cairn shows once what is new.

Cairn runs once per session. Starting it again — from the dock, a desktop action or a terminal — brings the open window forward with what you asked for.
