Click the computer at the bottom of the sidebar. You see the model, system and processor, how long it has been up and in use, then cards for battery health (condition, maximum capacity, cycles), the startup disk, memory, displays, security (disk encryption, firewall, Secure Boot, AppArmor, automatic updates) and software.

Everything is read on your computer, without administrator rights; what cannot be read says *Unknown*. **Hide Names** masks the computer's and your user name, and **Copy as Text** copies the page — handy for a support request.
