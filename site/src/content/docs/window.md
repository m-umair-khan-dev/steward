---
title: The window
description: Sidebar, header, path bar, the folder, and the preview pane.
order: 2
section: Start
---

- **Sidebar** (left): your workspaces, favourite places, tools, and other drives under *Locations*. The computer at the bottom shows free space; click it for [About This Computer](/docs/about-this-computer/). Collapse the sidebar with <kbd>F9</kbd>.
- **Header**: back and forward, the folder's name, grouping (none, kind, date), grid or list, sorting and card size, the preview pane, and a filter field.
- **Path bar**: click a part to go there, or press <kbd>Ctrl+L</kbd> and type a path or just a folder's name.
- **The folder**: folders as tinted glass folders, files as cards. Kind chips above narrow it to images, documents and so on.
- **Preview pane** (<kbd>Ctrl+Shift+P</kbd>): facts about the selection, and a folder's contents two levels deep.
