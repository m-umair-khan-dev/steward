---
title: Browse
description: Open folders, sort and group, filter, and show hidden files.
order: 3
section: Everyday
---

Double-click a folder or press <kbd>Enter</kbd> to open it; <kbd>Alt+←</kbd> and <kbd>Alt+→</kbd> go back and forward, <kbd>Alt+↑</kbd> goes up.

- **Grid or list**: <kbd>Ctrl+1</kbd> and <kbd>Ctrl+2</kbd>. Cairn remembers your choice for each folder.
- **Card size**: <kbd>Ctrl++</kbd> and <kbd>Ctrl+-</kbd>.
- **Group by kind** turns loose files into kind folders on screen — nothing moves on disk.
- **Filter** (<kbd>Ctrl+F</kbd>) narrows the folder to names containing what you type.
- **Hidden files**: <kbd>Ctrl+H</kbd>.

Every folder shows its item count and, once the index has seen it, its size.
