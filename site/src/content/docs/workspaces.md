---
title: Workspaces
description: One page for a project's folders and files, wherever they are.
order: 12
section: Tools
---

Press **+** next to *Workspaces* in the sidebar (or <kbd>Ctrl+Alt+N</kbd>), give it a name, then add folders and files: drag them onto the workspace, or right-click and choose *Add to*. Nothing moves on disk.

A workspace page shows each item with where it lives, in tabs for folders, images and documents. If you rename or move a folder, the workspace finds it again. If it is gone, *Locate…* points the workspace at its new place; if its drive is unplugged, the card says which drive.

<kbd>Alt+1</kbd> to <kbd>Alt+9</kbd> open your first nine workspaces.
