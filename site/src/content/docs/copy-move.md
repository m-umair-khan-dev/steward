title: Copy, move and drag
description: Copy and paste, drag and drop, and work with Files.
order: 6
section: Everyday
---

<kbd>Ctrl+C</kbd>, <kbd>Ctrl+X</kbd> and <kbd>Ctrl+V</kbd> copy, cut and paste — also between Cairn and Files.

Dragging moves items within one drive and copies them onto another; hold <kbd>Ctrl</kbd> to copy anyway. Long copies show their progress in the header; you can cancel them.

When a name is already taken, Cairn asks: **Skip**, **Keep Both** (the new item gets a free name), or **Replace** — which puts the existing item in the Trash, so Undo can bring it back.
