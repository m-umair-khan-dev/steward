---
title: Sidebar
description: Favourites, workspaces, tools and drives.
order: 8
section: Everyday
---

Right-click a favourite to move it up or down or hide it; *Restore Sidebar* in the main menu brings everything back. Drop files on a sidebar place to move them there. Drives and network shares appear under *Locations*, with an eject button.

When the sidebar is collapsed (<kbd>F9</kbd>), a narrow rail of icons stays; point at it to slide the sidebar out.
