---
title: Rename, Trash and Undo
description: Every file change can be undone.
order: 7
section: Everyday
---

- **Rename**: <kbd>F2</kbd>, type, <kbd>Enter</kbd>.
- **New folder**: <kbd>Ctrl+Shift+N</kbd>.
- **Move to Trash**: <kbd>Delete</kbd>. In the Trash, *Put Back* returns items to where they were.
- **Undo** (<kbd>Ctrl+Z</kbd>) and **Redo** (<kbd>Ctrl+Shift+Z</kbd>) work for every move, copy, rename, new folder, Tidy and Move to Trash — even after Steward restarts.

*Empty Trash* is the one thing that cannot be undone, so it always asks first.
